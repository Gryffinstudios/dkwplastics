Products and Sectors

At our Poly Packaging Factory, we take pride in offering a wide range of products that cater to various sectors and industries.
With our diverse product portfolio, we can meet the unique packaging needs of different businesses and sectors.
Here are some of the products we offer and the sectors they serve:

Resealable Ziplock bags

Discover versatile storage solutions with our unique Resealable Ziplock Bags, tailored to your needs. These Ziplocks are ideal for robust needs in jewelry, spare-parts, manufacturing, office organization, medical storage, and home use.

  • Plain Ziplock Bags
  • Ziplock Bags with Customized Print
  • Ziplock Bags with Patch Handles

Personalize your Ziplocks with our customizable features like patch handles and printed artwork to create a perfect fit for your requirements.

Food Grade Packaging​

We take pride in delivering government-certified food-grade packaging options, which strictly adhere to rigorous quality and safety standards. Our comprehensive range is tailored to diverse food products, consisting of films, inner bulk bags, and more. To name a few:

  • Tea, Flour, Sugar, Mixture Bags
  • Bakery Items
  • Bulk Dry Food Items

These solutions exceed industry requirements, enabling an extended shelf life. safeguarding freshness, providing protection for food items during prolonged storage and transportation.

Retail Textile Bags

Our retail textile bags are designed to be both stylish and functional, making them the perfect choice for businesses looking to make a positive impression.

  • Carrier Bags
  • Loop Handle
  • Kidney Cut Bags

Agriculture

Our agricultural packaging solutions are designed to withstand the demands of the industry.

  • Nursery Bag (with or without Perforation)
  • 3 Layer Film Film Supply for Lamination
  • 3 Layer Film Supply for Rain Guards

We offer durable and protective packaging for rubber estates, tea plantations, and other crops, ensuring that your products are safely transported and stored.

Garment Packaging

When it comes to garment packaging, including hanger bags, we specialize in providing solutions that merge practicality with elegance. Our range of garment packaging options is meticulously designed to ensure the safe and stylish presentation of your clothing.

  • Hanger Bags
  • Garment Covers
  • Envelope Bags

Whether it’s hanger bags, garment covers, or customized packaging, we understand the importance of making your clothing look its best. Our solutions not only protect your garments but also elevate their visual appeal, making them ready to shine in any retail or storage setting.

These versatile packaging solutions offer a burst of vibrant colors, adding visual appeal to your products. Whether you need to showcase your items, organize materials, or create eye-catching displays, our Multi- Color Polythene Tube/Reel Form packaging does it all with flair. Crafted with precision and quality, these packaging options provide both style and functionality, making them an ideal choice for a wide range of applications.

Gusseted Bags

Gusseted Bags, available in both Bottom Seal Side Gusset and Side Seal Bottom Gusset designs, offer exceptional versatility for various packaging needs. Their expandable side gussets enable you to accommodate bulkier or irregularly shaped items with ease, making them perfect for packaging textiles, clothing, food items, or promotional materials. Whether you need to store, display, or transport goods, our Gusseted Bags provide a reliable and adaptable solution to meet your requirements.
